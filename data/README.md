# Data availability

Respondent-level survey data are **not included** in this public repository.

The project used an anonymous Qualtrics survey and analyzed a final sample of 63 usable responses after data-quality screening. Even though the public analysis variables do not contain names or direct identifiers, row-level survey records are intentionally withheld to preserve participant privacy and follow good research-data practice.

To reproduce the analysis locally, place the private cleaned file at:

```text
data/GenAI_FINAL_SmartPLS_Ready.csv
```

Expected variables:

```text
PEOU1 PEOU2 PEOU3 PEOU4
PU1 PU2 PU3 PU4
SI1 SI2 SI3
BI1 BI2 BI3
AGE EXP
```

Aggregate results, publication tables, figures, and the final manuscript are available elsewhere in this repository.
