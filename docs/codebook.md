# Codebook

| Variable | Construct / role | Description | Scale / coding | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PEOU1 | Perceived Ease of Use | Learning GenAI for academic tasks is easy | 1-7 Likert | Davis (1989), adapted |
| PEOU2 | Perceived Ease of Use | Interaction is clear and understandable | 1-7 Likert | Davis (1989), adapted |
| PEOU3 | Perceived Ease of Use | Easy to become skillful | 1-7 Likert | Davis (1989), adapted |
| PEOU4 | Perceived Ease of Use | Overall ease of use | 1-7 Likert | Davis (1989), adapted |
| PU1 | Perceived Usefulness | Helps complete academic tasks more quickly | 1-7 Likert | Davis (1989), adapted |
| PU2 | Perceived Usefulness | Improves academic-task performance | 1-7 Likert | Davis (1989), adapted |
| PU3 | Perceived Usefulness | Increases academic productivity | 1-7 Likert | Davis (1989), adapted |
| PU4 | Perceived Usefulness | Overall usefulness for academic tasks | 1-7 Likert | Davis (1989), adapted |
| SI1 | Social Influence | Important people think GenAI should be used | 1-7 Likert | Venkatesh et al. (2012), adapted |
| SI2 | Social Influence | People influencing academic behavior support use | 1-7 Likert | Venkatesh et al. (2012), adapted |
| SI3 | Social Influence | Valued opinions support GenAI use | 1-7 Likert | Venkatesh et al. (2012), adapted |
| BI1 | Behavioral Intention | Intends to continue using GenAI | 1-7 Likert | Davis et al. (1989), adapted |
| BI2 | Behavioral Intention | Plans regular future use | 1-7 Likert | Davis et al. (1989), adapted |
| BI3 | Behavioral Intention | Expects use when appropriate and useful | 1-7 Likert | Davis et al. (1989), adapted |
| AGE | Control | Age in years | Numeric | Survey background item |
| EXP | Control | Prior GenAI experience | Ordered 1-5 | Survey background item |
| LEVEL | Descriptive | Academic level | Undergraduate / Master's / Doctoral / Other | Survey background item |
| FREQ | Descriptive | Frequency of GenAI use | Ordered category | Survey background item |
| TOOL | Descriptive | Preferred GenAI tool | Categorical | Survey background item |

## Construct abbreviations

- **PEOU** = Perceived Ease of Use
- **PU** = Perceived Usefulness
- **SI** = Social Influence
- **BI** = Behavioral Intention
